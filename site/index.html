How it works

Find, fill, check, submit

About five minutes from install to your first application. Turn autopilot off and everything stops.

  1. 1

    Find

    Reads SimplifyJobs' internship and new-grad lists, a company's job board, or a link you paste, and keeps the roles you qualify for.

  2. 2

    Fill

    Opens each application in a real browser and fills it from your resume and profile, the way you would.

  3. 3

    Check

    Every written answer cites the lines of your resume it used and is checked before it can go. Anything unsure waits for you.

  4. 4

    Submit

    Sends it once every answer checks out, within the limits you set, and tells you when it's in.

Safe by design

Safe to leave running

Autopilot is only useful if you can trust what it sends. These rules don't bend.

Never invents facts

Answers come from your resume and profile. A written answer that can't show where it came from is held, not sent.

Holds instead of guessing

Work authorization, sponsorship and demographic answers come only from what you've confirmed. The first application on each site waits for your OK.

Caps you set

A daily limit and a per-company limit, plus any limit a company's posting states. Autopilot stops at each one.

Local-first

Your resume, answers and keys stay in a folder on your computer. No account, no Jobsmith server, no tracking. It talks only to job sites, the AI you pick and your own email.

Works with no AI

Contact details, work authorization and questions you've answered before come from your profile and past answers. Written answers wait for you, or use smith, a free model that runs on your computer.

Skills

Turn on the parts you want

Apply is the core. Switch the others on in Skills.

  • Apply

    Autopilot for applications, plus a Chrome side panel: autofill, resume match, and help with open questions.

  • Your voice

    Learns how you write from your essays and approved answers, on your computer, so answers sound like you.

  • Use it from your agent

    Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Cursor can write the answers: jobsmith skills install. Jobsmith still checks each one.

  • Outreach drafts

    Referral and recruiter messages to people you know at each company. You send them; Jobsmith never does.

  • Inbox and follow-ups

    Reads replies from Gmail or Outlook (read-only), enters emailed codes, and drafts a follow-up when a company goes quiet.

  • Interview notesnext

    Notes, the questions you were asked, and a thank-you draft, transcribed on your computer with everyone's consent.

  • Interview prepnext

    Practice questions and STAR stories built from the posting and your resume.

  • Offerslater

    Compare offers side by side, with talking points for negotiating.

Install

One command, then run jobsmith

It sets up Python and a browser for Jobsmith and nothing else. No git, compiler or admin rights needed.

curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rawcache/Jobsmith/main/install.sh | sh

Paste it into Terminal, then run jobsmith. It asks what you're applying for and reads your resume.

Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

Nothing. Jobsmith is free and open source under the MIT license. Gemini's free tier handles most days, and when its daily limit runs out, smith can take over on your own computer. Paid Claude or OpenAI keys work too.

Which job sites does it apply on?

Greenhouse and Ashby end to end; Lever and most single-page forms are filled generically. Workday, iCIMS and other multi-page sites are filled page by page and stop at review, and you sign in yourself.

Will it apply to jobs I don't qualify for?

No. Roles too senior for you, asking for more experience than you have, or paying under your minimum are left out before a browser opens. You can see them under "Left out" and apply anyway.

What won't it do?

Type your passwords, create accounts, solve CAPTCHAs, send messages for you, automate LinkedIn, or answer questions your profile doesn't cover. Those come to you, under the bell.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere but your computer. Jobsmith has no servers and no tracking. It connects only to the job sites it applies on, the AI you choose, and your own email if you connect it.

Is it made by Simplify?

No. Jobsmith can read SimplifyJobs' public internship and new-grad lists, but it isn't affiliated with Simplify or any employer.
