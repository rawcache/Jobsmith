What it does

Built for students applying to software, hardware, data and AI roles, where the same twenty questions get asked a hundred times.

Applies on its own

Point it at SimplifyJobs' internship and new-grad lists, a company, or a link. It checks you qualify, fills the form in a real browser and submits. Turn autopilot off and everything stops.

Never makes things up

Answers come from your resume and profile, and written answers cite the lines they used. When it isn't sure, it holds the application for you instead of guessing.

Stays on your computer

Your resume, answers and keys live in a folder on your machine. Write with Gemini's free tier, or smith, a model that runs locally and never sends anything out.

Pick the skills you want

Jobsmith is a set of parts. Apply is the core; switch the others on in Skills.

  • Apply

    Autopilot for applications, plus a Chrome side panel: autofill, resume match, and Write with Jobsmith on open questions.

  • Your voice

    Learns how you write from your essays and approved answers, on your computer. Answers in your words with no AI at all, and keeps any AI sounding like you.

  • Use it from your agent

    Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Cursor can be the brain: jobsmith skills install. Jobsmith checks every answer before it's sent.

  • Outreach drafts

    Referral and recruiter messages to people you know at each company. You send them; Jobsmith never does.

  • Inbox and follow-ups

    Reads replies from Gmail or Outlook (read-only), enters emailed codes, and drafts a follow-up when a company goes quiet.

  • Interview notesnext

    Notes, the questions you were asked, and a thank-you draft, transcribed on your computer with everyone's consent.

  • Interview prepnext

    Practice questions and STAR stories built from the posting and your resume, before the interview.

  • Offerslater

    Compare offers side by side, with talking points for negotiating.

How it works

About five minutes from install to your first application.

  1. Install

    One command. It sets up Python and a browser for Jobsmith and nothing else.

  2. Run jobsmith

    Pick what you're applying for and which AI to use, then drop in your resume. The dashboard opens for anything it couldn't read.

  3. Turn on autopilot

    It applies to your best matches every few hours. CAPTCHAs and sign-ins come to you, under the bell.

apply to nvidia and amd hardware internships

Good to know

What it costs

Nothing. Gemini's free tier handles most days, and when its daily limit runs out, smith can take over on your own computer. Paid Claude or OpenAI keys work too.

What it won't do

Type your passwords, create accounts, solve CAPTCHAs, send messages for you, or answer questions your profile doesn't cover.

Where it applies

Greenhouse and Ashby end to end; Lever and most single-page forms filled generically. Workday, iCIMS and other multi-page sites are filled page by page and stop at review; you sign in yourself.

Try it safely
