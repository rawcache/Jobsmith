# Outreach drafts

Who you know at each company you're applying to (from your LinkedIn connections export), and a short message
for each: a referral ask, a note to a recruiter, or a thank-you after an interview. Drafts only: you copy one
and send it from your own account. Jobsmith never sends messages.

For an AI agent: `jobsmith_people_at` lists the user's connections at a company; `jobsmith_draft_outreach`
drafts a message. Show the draft to the user; never send it.
