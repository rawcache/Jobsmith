# Interview notes (planned)

Transcribes an interview on your computer (a local speech-to-text model, nothing uploaded), then gives you a
summary, the questions you were asked, what to follow up on, and a thank-you note draft.

It asks you to confirm everyone on the call agreed to be recorded: in many places that's the law. It takes notes
for you afterwards; it does not feed you answers during the interview.
